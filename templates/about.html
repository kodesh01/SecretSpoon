Meet Our Founder

Hello, I’m Kavitha Rajan, the founder of Sundari Foods. My journey began in my grandmother's kitchen, where the aroma of freshly ground spices and the crackle of mustard seeds were everyday music. After years of making pickles and masalas for friends and family, I decided to bring that homemade love to every household.

What started as a small home business is now a brand trusted by thousands. Our promise? No preservatives, no shortcuts — just honest, traditional flavors that speak for themselves.
